# RIPA: Act 3 Part 6
## Session 90 (2026-10-13)
### Inside the Nexus Of Creation (Three Realities Deep)

We have been stuck for hours - or is it days? - in the Astral Sea, trying to figure out how on earth we "break" this reality and move on, or even if it *is* the 'real reality' (Alustriel might've been wrong about the number of "layers"), in which case we have to figure out how to reach 'good Vecna' (Vance).

Thorin has seen Vecna's notes, implying time/fate are the lie.

Back inside the got-eating titan, within that strange 'temple'/gazebo, we look at the circle where versions of Vecna have occasionally appeared. The walls have the various gems, and there are the sigils of each school of magic, appear around us. For example:

* Conjuration - movement, moving yourself or bringing in things
* Divination - time?
* Abjuration - protect, ward, prevent
* And so-on.

Thorin: ARC 21. Necromancy - rejuvenates or withers; speed up or slow down ageing. Divination - that could even let you see the future, by asking the Gods, thinks Penny. So, Francesc cautiously shoves, rather than striking, that glyph. **The tile is on cracks!** It's as if sound is distant suddenly. Muffled sounds. Cracks appear through - reality itself!

(So time-fate - that *one* is enough. "They" are related; a form of the same thing).

A strange greyish-blue nothing appears behind. Not like the Astral sea; just _nothing_. Cracks propagate - **reality shatters**. And the six of us are just floating!

"So what *now?*" 'thinks' Mynos.

Penny tries to respond by saying, "I'm not sure!" and Thorin is also trying to opine about time being destroyed now, perhaps. We're looking around, trying to figure out if "normal physics" applies. Thorin tries to throw a small flask; but it's bizarre - every "frame" of Thorin moving is visible to Penny (low PER roll); a "tube" of flasks appear in a row. Francesc (high PER roll) on the other hand seems instantaneous moments. Everything happening at the same time, but discrete. Thorin (even higher roll) sees the flask go in *multiple* directions, as if there are multiple fates.

Maybe a spell? Divination school? Thorin could use Foresight, but that's L9. Francesc tries the rod - Commune - perhaps... Gaia?

"Where and when are we?" she asks, not thinking too clearly - the spell should strictly only allow a "yes" or "no" answer. Nonetheless - a voice answers.

"The second half of your question is nonsensical. The first half is up to you." The voice sounds both ancient and young. "What is your next question?"

At Thorin's suggestion, Francesc asks how we find the "good" version of Vecna.

"To do that, you'll have to get past *me*. Is that what you really want? What is your last question?"

That's not Gaia... Is that - who is that? Vecna?! "Why must we get past you?" asks Francesc.

"Why?! Because I'm bored!"

### Time Dragon's Lair

Initiative!

* Francesc: 21
* Penny: 18
* Thorin: 15

...but - wait, what?! - no Urth, Flurth or Mynos. We're quite a long way apart from each other, suddenly appearing at the exits of three tunnels - one each - that all emerge into the same massive cavern, the roof of which goes up at least 200ft. We are, in essence, at points of a triangle centred at the middle of this cavern's floor. In the centre is a cluster of some strange crystals, that look almost made of light. Coiled around it is a vast dragon, with the same colouring!

**Francesc realises instinctively that *this* is the entity to whom she was just talking**.

Francesc's dragon sensing ability fires off. Yes! It's a dragon! And clearly hostile; its tone had been unmistakeable. She brings up Hunter's Mark:

* An adult Time Dragon
* Resistant to: Nothing
* Immune to: Nothing
* Vulnerable to: Nothing

She fires. A near-miss... Fires again. Hit! 15 damage. She runs a distance down the tunnel entranceway, out of sight, in case of breath weapon attack.

Penny considers a few options and given the unknown capabilities and strength of this creature, she tries Banishment. CHA 20 to save. Nat 20 on its save, so no joy there... So all she can do is light the sun sword and get ready to attack physically.

Thorin considers Shape Change... He thinks he can *just*  match this creature... For now though he casts Earthbind. STR 19 save. Its wings *start* to crumple inwards as the spell takes effect, but then it *choose* to save and the wings come back. So instead, he Moonlight Steps right up in front of it, balancing on a crystal right in front of its face!

The dragon calls out, "Elf, why are you hiding? Come out and play!" then looks at Thorin and exhales a shimmering cone of light that expands outwards and away behind him. CON save 18; 25 force damage... But he is also - desynchronised. Everything seems strange - staccato - it would almost be akin to being poisoned, except for Heroes Feast, which seems to buffer some of the effects.

Francesc runs back in and fires again, getting a decisive hit for 13 damage; it halves that to 6 by reaction, and teleports to the cavern side, near where Thorin's tunnel emerged. Penny's view of it is obscured by the cluster of giant crystals around which it was previously coiled. Francesc moves to get a good angle - but it's not enough; the attack misses.

Penny runs as far as she can to get within range of the dragon and once again tries Banishment. And once again, the dragon saves! Rats.

Thorin is slightly "out of time". Heroes Feast helps him focus enough to attack, should he wish. He tries his Earthbind again. Nat 20 on its save! It *chuckles* and says, "It's not my doing, but the three of you appear to be stuck in a time loop of some sort..."

A bad, *bad* Time Dragon pun. "I think that, now, I *want* to kill you" says Penny.

Thorin jumps over and tries to bonus-action attack. Nope! Miss. He makes his save against the time effects - Penny's aura helping, but he would've made it anyway.

The dragon gets its breath weapon back... It lines up Thorin and Penny... 60ft cone. CON saves - we both fail... And now we are *both* affected by the strange time effect. At least Heroes Feast helps... But it's **45** force damage! We are able to reduce that by 1d4 - 3 for Thorin, 4 for Penny -> 42, 41 damage taken.

Francesc runs in, fires - wondering why there doesn't seem to be much sound of battle going on other than hers?! - and hits, doing 31 damage; she throws in Stasis Strike - 5 damage more - but it halves to 18, saves against the effect and it teleports *again*. Now it's hidden from her by the crystals, and *right next* to Penny! She moves to get a line of sight again, then fires once more... But it *just* misses.

Penny hits with the Sunsword, 26 damage via Staggering Smite; it makes its save. She hits again with a *much* better damage roll - 24, and it reacts with a rending attack in return. It has advantage since she is out of the "time stream" and hits, dealing 21 damage. "Tis but a scratch!" says Penny. She is *not* able to escape the strange time effect from the breath weapon.

From Thorin - a *third* attempt Earthbind! STR 19 save; 19, matches DC and saves. So he gives up on that and Wildshapes into his Quetz form, and shakes off the breath weapon effect.

The Time Dragon activates some kind of time slowing effect; Penny is still influenced by its changes to the passage of time, so she ends up with (in effect) zero movement speed. It decides to fly - which gives Thorin and Penny an opportunity attack each. Penny hits, which is a nat 20, but only 26 damage; but Thorin sadly misses.

It flies over to Francesc and attacks! The first misses, but the next hits, dealing 22 damage total (accounting for Francesc's resistances); but she Reacts to become Resistant to the slashing damage too, so ends up at 13 total. A third attack hits, but the various Resistances halve the damage down to 10.

Francesc fires point-blank, hits; it *chooses* to save against Stasis Strike. 23 damage overall. It reacts with another attack, but it misses. She fires again - another hit - 12 damage. She realises it's reacted already, uses Nature's Veil to disappear and retreats back down the same corridor.

Penny can't see the dragon, can't move, and doesn't make the save against the time effect.

Thorin brings down L5 Moonbeam - 32 damage, CON save - it *chooses* to succeed. It takes 16 damage. He tries his Sacred Flame cantrip (Spellfire Spark) - which works, dealing 20 damage, the save being failed. It uses its last reaction in response to teleport 30ft straight up in the air. Well Penny can't *reach* it, but at least she can *see* it now!

The dragon says, "well that was fun! But I've had quite enough. I think it's time for you to go!"  - it flies up a further 80ft up, so it's 110ft in the air, then looks down menacingly at us.

Francesc moves so she can get a view. **She shouts, "If it's time for us to go, does this mean we can get past you to Vecna?"**

"A deal's a deal."

And suddenly, we're elsewhere

### Vecna's - lair? Maybe?

Initiative re-rolled:

* Francesc 27
* Penny 16
* Thorin 14

We're in a smaller cavern, lower roof, with Thorin-Quetz filling most of the space! There are two dead creatures here; one looks like possibly a king, hands and face stained in blood. The other is a death knight, but it seems to have no face! They're stood up, though...

...dead - but not dead! Undead, in fact! Some kind of aura emanates from the king, a Regent maybe?, which seems to galvanise the Death Knight somehow. It moves next to Francesc and releases a destructive wave of necrotic energy. CON saves all around:

* Francesc 10
* Thorin 32
* Penny 10

17 thunder damage, 19 necrotic, 36 total. Penny and Francesc are knocked prone, too. It moves past us but Penny gets an opportunity attack in, dealing 24 damage; it is Sapped, too; it stops right to her. Thorin-Quetz uses Sentinel to attack with a bite; but it *just* misses; Francesc tries too just jabbing physically with her bow, and hits - 2 damage!

Francesc stands up, puts Hunter's Mark on the faceless undead and:

* Resistant: Nothing
* Immune: Exhaustion, Fear, Posion(ed), Necrotic

...attacks, nat 20! 29 damage. Second attack - with Stasis Strike, 22 damage. The Stasis Strike takes effect. She moves away, towards a door or wall of some kind at one end of this odd chamber. PER check - high. There is a strange corridor here, sapphire and ruby lights, crystalline partitions of some kind and things moving beyond. There's a strange, 20ft tall, lurking shape just beyond the *second* of two of these strange partitions. She wonders if this is a strange kind of prison. That strange creature moves forward, touches a ruby by the strange "glass" partition and - disappears! And *then* she sees a frightened-looking young man, quite handsome, appear in the same general area; look around in a hurry; then touches another ruby and disappears also. She's pretty sure it's young Vecna. She shouts it out to us!

The faceless releases a Necrotic Wave. 13 damage for Francesc, 13 for Thorin who both made saves; Penny takes the full force of it.

Penny swipes at the Regent but misses, then hits decisively on the second hit, using Blinding Smite. Solid damage; 46; but it makes its save and is not blinded.

Thorin-Quetz casts L5 Mass Cure Wounds. 32hp all round. He shouts to the rest of us - "try to keep progressing forward!" - then goes to bite the regent, flanking Penny, hits - 27! He teleports forward a little, grabs Penny and *throws* her forward next to Francesc.

The Regent runs up to Thorin and attacks three times with a blade. Miss, hit (19 damage taken), hit (18 damage taken).

Francesc is torn between maybe healing, or trying out that ruby. She can't really see either of the two Undead, so drops Hunter's Mark and opts for healing, using a L4 concentration healing spirit spell that covers her and Penny. She gains 11hp straight away, and Penny gains 10. She looks closely at the ruby by the strange crystal door. She sees her reflection; PER 18; it is mimicking her movements! It's not actually her - and suddenly, a Mirror Shade appears! A kind of dark reflection of herself.

Thorin has been encouraging her to touch the ruby... She now does - and appears on the other side of the partition. She sees the Mirror Shade and tries to use Sacred Flame, but it fails - magic does not appear to cross this translucent barrier. INT save 14 - Penny's aura isn't crossing the partition either. She realises that she can't hear anything through, or sense anything from, the other side of the barrier either. All she can do is see things there. There's also an unpleasant psychic "hum"! It's not damaging, but it feels like the walls are bouncing and amplifying something over and over. She will be a little distracted within this space.

At that moment, a huge creature - she thinks it's the strange thing she saw earlier - appears at the end of the short corridor, squeezes its way in and heads right for her! It seems to *absorb* light and as it gets close, she feels a strange, unpleasant, disruptive energy around her... It points at her... WIS save - with luck point, only 10 - she takes **44 necrotic** but there aren't other effects thanks to Heroes Feast and she reacts to half the damage, to 22.

And then, to her surprise, she sees the young Vecna appear behind. "Don't let it see you! Use the crystals! Keep running! The blue take you to red, the red to blue, except the one behind you! **I've been playing Hide And Seek for hundreds of years!**"

(End of act 3, part 6)
